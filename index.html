Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I create an account?

Use Create account on the welcome page. You can register with your email and password. Google sign-in is also available when enabled.

Will my study progress sync across devices?

At this stage, planner and tracker data is saved in the browser on the device you use. Cross-device cloud sync is not active yet.

Are all questions official JEE PYQs?

No. Questions marked Sample or Mission IIT Original are practice questions, not verified official previous-year questions. Official PYQs will be labelled with their verified year and source when added.

How do I report incorrect study material?

Choose Study material support below and include the subject, chapter, and a short description of the issue.

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check your internet connection, confirm that you are using the website's https link, and use Forgot password if needed. If the issue continues, send a support request.

Contact Mission IIT

Choose the type of help you need. Your email app will open with a prepared message.
